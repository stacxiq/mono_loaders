## 0.1.0

- Initial release with 12 monochrome loaders: Wire Sphere, Dot Globe, Liquid Orb, Arc Ring, Tick Dial, Orbit Atom, Shape Morph, Wire Cube, Helix, Infinity Trace, Dot Matrix, Square Tunnel.
- Determinate and indeterminate modes, smooth progress, speed/thickness controls, semantics.
- `MonoLoaderType` enum and `MonoLoader` base class for custom loaders.
